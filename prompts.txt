RHINO — Proposed Rebrand Direction
Brand Positioning

I would position Rhino as:

A strong, modern insurance brokerage and risk advisory brand focused on helping individuals and organisations make confident protection decisions.

Brand personality

Rhino should feel:

Strong
Reliable
Intelligent
Professional
Modern
Approachable
Calm under pressure

The key difference from Mahogany:

Mahogany felt corporate and established. Rhino should feel strong, strategic and protective.

1. Proposed Brand Concept

The rhinoceros gives us an excellent metaphor, but we should use it subtly.

Not:

🦏 Safari
🦏 Wildlife
🦏 African tourism

Instead, the visual idea is:

Strength + Protection + Confidence

Proposed brand statement
Strong Protection. Smarter Decisions.

This is currently my favourite direction.

It works for both sides of the business:

Insurance Broking
→ Protection

Consulting / Advisory
→ Smarter decisions

Other options:

Protection You Can Move Forward With.
Strength Behind Your Protection.
Protection Built Around What Matters.
Confidence Through Better Protection.

My recommendation:

Primary tagline

Strong Protection. Smarter Decisions.

Image Strategy 📸

This project should have a lot of imagery, just like you wanted for Mahogany.

But Rhino's images should feel different.

Image style

Use:

African professionals
Realistic corporate meetings
Business owners
Vehicles and transport
Warehouses
Commercial buildings
Families
Professionals reviewing documents

Avoid:

❌ Excessive handshakes
❌ Generic smiling call-centre agents
❌ Random skyscrapers
❌ Too many shield icons
❌ Literal rhino imagery everywhere 😅

Rhino imagery rule

The rhino is a metaphor for the brand, not the content of every section.

Let's treat this as:

RHINO DIGITAL REBRAND — VERSION 0.1
